# Research To Script

Use this when a from-scratch video needs factual grounding before writing. This
is not a raw research dump; it is a conversion pass from sources into verified
story material.
Read `knowledge_plan.md` first so the research pass uses the selected style,
source guidance, extracted lessons, asset rules, and QC checks.

## Research Outputs

Create or update:

- `research_notes.md`
- `script.md`
- `asset_list.md` entries for evidence visuals
- `qc_report.md` factual-risk notes when needed

## Research Rules

- Use primary sources, official docs, public datasets, credible reporting, or
  clearly labeled expert commentary.
- Record source URL, title, creator/publisher, retrieval date, and claim used.
- Separate verified fact, interpretation, and creative narration.
- Do not present generated images as real evidence.
- Do not use screenshots, maps, charts, logos, music, or stock assets until
  rights and attribution requirements are known.
- When sources conflict, keep the contradiction in the notes and script the
  uncertainty honestly.

## Converting Research Into Script

- Turn timelines into cause-and-effect beats.
- Turn stats into comparisons, charts, or visual metaphors.
- Turn abstract systems into named actors, decisions, incentives, or constraints.
- Put the strongest sourced claim near the hook or early proof beat.
- Keep citations attached to the beat that uses them.

## QC

- Every factual claim in the voiceover has a source.
- Any uncertainty is labeled rather than smoothed away.
- Evidence visuals are rights-safe or marked as pending.
- The final script is story-shaped, not a list of facts.
