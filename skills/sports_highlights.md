# Sports Highlights

## Purpose

Guide selection and assembly of sports clips, replays, commentary, and momentum.

## Use When

- Creating highlight reels, player edits, match recaps, or social sports clips.
- Syncing commentary, crowd audio, and action.

## Decision Rules

- Lead with the strongest action or stakes.
- Preserve anticipation before major plays when it makes the payoff better.
- Use replay, slow motion, or freeze frames to clarify skill.
- Keep commentary lines that add emotion, identity, or context.

## Technique Cards

Add cards for replay punch-in, player spotlight, commentary bridge, slow-mo impact, and scoreboard context.

## QC Rules

- The viewer can follow who did what.
- Slow motion does not hide the key action.
- Music and effects do not bury important commentary or crowd response.
