# Editorial Brain Pipeline

Before the ten passes, the Brain validates the checked consolidated knowledge base,
synthesizes a source-capability-aware TasteProfile, resolves contextual
principles/recipes/gates, and compiles them into the typed policy used by
selects, cuts, pacing and global search. Base and taste fingerprints
participate in reproducibility and decision provenance.

The planner executes ten versioned passes: understanding, selects, assembly,
story refinement, fine cut, audio/picture, B-roll/cutaways, rhythm,
presentation intent, and compile. Each pass stores its artifact plus a
structured diff from the preceding pass.

Media analysis derives structural candidate times from frames, shots, words,
phrases, pauses, motion/action, transients, beats, reactions and handles.
Semantic calls receive those verified candidates. Selects retain top-K
alternatives. Beam/DP search creates several complete assemblies before fine
cut and AV refinement.

Analysis includes scene detection, representative frames, sharpness,
luminance/histograms, optical flow, streaming RMS/peaks/transients,
conservative pause classification, beat/bar tracking, cross-source audio
correlation, contact sheets and structurally bounded subshots. Source dialogue
becomes phrase-backed beats; unprotected dead air is omitted and protected
reaction/dramatic pauses become explicit beats. Script/voice-over mode aligns
script tokens to narration words and records reveal timing.

Fine cut enumerates structural in/out pairs. AV planning can select hard AV,
J/L, reaction/dialogue continuation, bridge, ambience, music-led,
intentional-silence, or narration-over-picture relationships. Supporting
picture gets a motivated proof/reaction/cutaway/montage role. Transitions remain
cuts unless a measured time jump and narrative function motivate a dissolve.

The final gate validates evidence references, canonical times, media, locks,
project invariants, patch revision, supported operations, render compilation,
and bounded integration render/QC evidence.
