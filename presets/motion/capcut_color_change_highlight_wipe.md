# CapCut Color-Change Highlight Wipe

Use this preset for a short premium title, quote, or claim where a highlight bar
changes the text color as it passes.

## Pattern

- Build base text on the controlled background.
- Duplicate the text above a custom-sized highlight bar.
- Invert or change the duplicate text color.
- Compound the duplicate text plus highlight bar.
- Add a `Split` mask rotated around `90` degrees.
- Keyframe the mask from fully left to fully right.
- Ease both mask keyframes.

## QC

- Base and highlighted text layers align perfectly.
- The wipe starts and ends outside the highlighted phrase.
- Both text-color states preserve strong contrast.
- Ordinary captions are removed or repositioned if they duplicate the designed text.
