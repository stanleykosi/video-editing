# After Effects Null Camera Scene

Use this preset pattern when a Reel segment needs spatial motion across icons, counters, paths, or proof objects.

## Suggested Values

- Camera setup: camera plus orbit null trimmed to the scene range.
- Layer setup: convert only layers that should respond to the camera into 3D layers.
- Motion start: begin camera/null movement a few frames before the visible beat when static starts feel dead.
- Easing: Easy Ease plus speed graph shaping; avoid linear camera motion.

## Rules

- Use nulls for grouped scene motion when direct layer keyframes would conflict.
- Keep backgrounds 2D if 3D camera motion reveals unwanted edges.
- Keep one focal target per camera move.
- Check captions and text during the camera move at phone size.
