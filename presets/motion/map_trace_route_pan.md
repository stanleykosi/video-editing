# Map Trace Route Pan

Use this preset for route, region, or location explanation sequences.

## Pattern

- Map base.
- Trace/outline PNG or shape.
- Feathered mask reveal.
- Origin marker.
- Directional dotted arrow or route wipe.
- Destination marker.
- Compound/nest.
- Eased pan, zoom, optional slight rotation, then fade or move aside.

## Starting Values

- Route reveal: `2 seconds`.
- Simple route: `1 second`.
- Complex route: up to `4 seconds`.
- Mask feather: small enough to soften the edge without blurring the route.

## QC

- Geography is truthful and sourceable.
- Origin, route, and destination are readable.
- Captions and markers do not cover key labels.
