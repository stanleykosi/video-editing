# Live-Action Text Integration

Use this preset when title, date, location, product, or proof text should belong
inside the shot instead of appearing as a separate card.

## Settings

- Placement: negative space first; avoid faces, hands, products, UI, lamps, and
  evidence.
- Font role: match scene tone before choosing motion.
- Color: match warm/cool temperature while preserving contrast.
- Motion: static, soft fade, or scene tracking only when the text should attach to
  the camera or object movement.
- Behind-subject text: bake the text into a prepared base, then place a clean
  foreground cutout above it only after timing is stable.
- Captions: assign a separate safe region or suppress duplicate captions.
- Hold: long enough to read at final size without overstaying the emotional beat.

## Use When

- A cinematic, documentary, ad, or social shot has clean space for text.
- The text adds time, place, claim, proof, or emotion without covering the subject.
- The text should appear behind the subject and the cutout edge is clean enough.

## Avoid When

- The frame is too busy for readable integrated text.
- A separate title card would be clearer.
- Tracking would add jitter or distract from the performance.
- The subject cutout covers too much of the word for it to remain readable.

## QC

- Text sits in negative space or tracks cleanly.
- Font and color feel native to the scene.
- Captions and scene text do not collide.
- Readability holds at phone size and final delivery size.
