# Center Subject Tracking

Use this preset for short-form talking-head clips where the speaker moves and the crop needs energy.

## Suggested Values

- Statement zoom: 108-125% scale.
- Tracking keyframes: every 4-12 frames during visible movement, or at movement turns for slower footage.
- Auto tracking: use face/body/hand tracking only while the target remains visible.
- Sticker tracking: position the sticker relationship first, then start tracking.
- Hold after zoom: 8-18 frames.
- Framing: preserve face, hands, captions, and important props.

## Rules

- Keep the speaker near the center guide unless text or proof needs intentional space.
- Use zooms for important statements, not filler lines.
- Leave room around the body when shooting or cropping so zooms do not cut off the subject.
- Shorten or replace auto tracking with manual keyframes when the target turns
  away, becomes occluded, or drifts.
