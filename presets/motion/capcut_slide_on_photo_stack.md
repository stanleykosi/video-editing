# CapCut Slide-On Photo Stack

Use when talking-head footage needs supporting photos in planned negative space.

## Starting Values

- Speaker framing: rule-of-thirds or intentionally off-center.
- Photo entrance: simple slide-in from the side.
- Calm slide duration: around 1.0-1.4 seconds before shortening for fast shorts.
- Stagger: offset each photo entrance so the viewer registers one image at a time.
- Exit: matching slide-out or fade before the next concept.

## Avoid When

- The speaker was centered and photos would cover face, captions, product, or
  proof.
