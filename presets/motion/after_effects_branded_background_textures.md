# After Effects Branded Background Textures

Use this preset family for clean branded Reel backgrounds.

## Suggested Values

- Grid opacity: around 20-30%.
- Venetian Blinds completion: around 20% for subtle line texture.
- Noise Alpha: low enough to read as texture, not compression damage.
- Vignette: enough to focus the frame without crushing edges.

## Rules

- Use a limited brand palette across all segments.
- Texture stays behind the foreground idea.
- Trim background and texture layers to the intended segment.
- Check text, icons, and captions over every background variation.
