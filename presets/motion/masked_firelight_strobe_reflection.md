# Masked Firelight Strobe Reflection

Use when a visible fire, neon, screen, or practical light should create local
movement on nearby surfaces.

## Starting Values

- Layer: duplicated base, adjustment layer, or colored light overlay.
- Effect: strobe/flicker applied only inside the mask.
- Mask: circle/ellipse or custom shape over the reflected surface.
- Feather: high or near-maximum when the surface has no hard boundary.
- Blend/opacity: subtle Screen/Add/opacity lift, weaker than the source light.

## Use When

- The light source is visible or clearly implied.
- The reflection helps an inserted fire or glow feel integrated.

## Avoid When

- The flicker covers faces, captions, proof, UI, products, or main action.
- The mask edge remains visible during playback.
