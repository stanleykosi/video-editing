# CapCut Locked-Camera Split Mask Illusion

Use for disappear-behind-object, peek-out, or clone effects filmed from a locked
camera.

## Starting Values

- Capture: lock camera, exposure, focus, and white balance.
- Base: clean plate or first take.
- Overlay: action or clone take.
- Mask: Split mask aligned to a real object edge.
- Sound: add foley only after visual timing works.

## Avoid When

- Auto camera changes, moving shadows, or camera movement reveal the composite.
