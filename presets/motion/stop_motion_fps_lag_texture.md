# Stop-Motion FPS Lag Texture

Use this preset late in an infographic build to give smooth motion a handmade,
editorial cadence.

## Starting Values

- CapCut FPS Lag speed: around `40`.
- Remotion/AE equivalent: test `8-15 fps` posterize-style cadence.
- Apply after timing is locked.

## QC

- Text, map labels, chart values, and source documents remain readable.
- Captions are stable or intentionally stylized and readable.
- SFX still feel synced to visible motion.
- Dense sections are bypassed or reduced if readability suffers.
