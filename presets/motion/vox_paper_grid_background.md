# Vox-Inspired Paper Grid Background

Use this preset for editorial explainer graphics that need a warm analog base.

## Starting Values

- Canvas: match delivery aspect before layout, often `16:9`.
- Paper scale: `115-120%` if edges do not cover the frame.
- Warmth: slight temperature lift toward warm/off-white.
- Grid opacity: `10-20%`.
- Paper edge/doodle texture opacity: `15-25%`.

## QC

- Grid does not compete with charts, maps, labels, or captions.
- Paper texture stays compression-clean.
- Foreground text remains readable at final size.
