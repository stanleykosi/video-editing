# CapCut Chapter Card Slide Interrupt

Use for numbered tips, essay sections, or tutorial resets where a full-screen
card creates a useful pattern interrupt.

## Starting Values

- Background: project-owned color, gradient, or designed card.
- Canvas: match delivery ratio, such as 1920x1080 or 1080x1920.
- Background entrance: simple slide or wipe.
- Text entrance: restrained zoom, fade, or pop.
- Exit: animate both text and background out before body footage resumes.

## Avoid When

- A small label would be faster or the card only repeats the speaker.
