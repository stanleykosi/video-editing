# Premium Brand Intro Sequence

Use this preset for reusable show, podcast, creator-brand, course, or social
series intros where the bumper has a real trust job.

## Structure

- Beat 1: brand/show/series name.
- Beat 2: external proof, authority, community, or recognizable niche trust cue.
- Beat 3: perceived value or viewer outcome.
- Handoff: clean transition into the body content.

## Timing

- Social/Reels/Instagram: about 3-5 seconds.
- Long-form podcast/show: about 10-15 seconds maximum.
- Text flicker/font-change accents: 3-9 frames only when readability holds.
- 3D camera or logo movement: keep the final readable brand/outcome frame before handoff.

## QC

- Brand, proof, and outcome are each readable.
- Motion serves one focus at a time.
- The intro is trimmed to finished animation, not padded build time.
- Removing the intro would make the brand/package weaker, not clearer.
