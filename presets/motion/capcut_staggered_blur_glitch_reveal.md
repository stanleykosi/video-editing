# CapCut Staggered Blur Glitch Text Reveal

Use this preset for a short stylized phrase where words enter in a staggered
social reveal with a brief blur and glitch cue.

## Starting Values

- Blade pre-reveal: around `2` frames from the start.
- Pre-reveal color: one intentional accent, such as red, only for the brief lead-in.
- Blur: around `25` for the heavier reveal segment, around `4` as the resolving segment.
- Sound: one glitch cue trimmed to the reveal duration.

## QC

- Staggered words preserve one reading order.
- Blur clears before the phrase needs to be read.
- The glitch cue ends with the final reveal and stays below speech.
- The effect is reserved for selected title or emphasis moments.
