# Premiere Adjustment Zoom-Blur

Use this preset for selected short-form section starts after the rough cut, captions, and body visuals already work.

## Suggested Values

- Zoom-out scale: 130% to 100%.
- Blur: Gaussian Blur 3 to 0.
- Optional push-in: 100% to 115% after the settle.
- Easing: fast start, slow settle; smooth later push.
- Range: adjustment layer covers only the intended section.

## Rules

- Do not use the zoom to hide weak pacing.
- Confirm captions, faces, UI, products, and proof remain visible through the full move.
- Duplicate the adjustment layer only onto sections that need flow or emphasis.
- Disable the effect during review once to confirm the edit still works without polish.
