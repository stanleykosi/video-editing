# Document Highlight Focus Pan

Use this preset when a source document, screenshot, or article needs highlighted
evidence.

## Pattern

- Source document/image.
- Warm or match adjustment.
- Highlight rectangle or underscore behind text.
- Directional wipe reveal.
- Compound/nest.
- Eased pan or zoom to highlighted line.
- Hold for reading.

## Starting Values

- Highlight wipe: `8-24 frames` for short phrases.
- Hold: long enough to read the highlighted words at final size.
- Blend: darken/multiply-style treatment when available.

## QC

- Highlight does not hide source wording.
- Enough context remains visible.
- Private or sensitive details are approved or removed.
