# CapCut Turbulent Wide Angle Shake Transition

## Use When

- A beat-matched action edit needs a high-energy transition pass.
- Turbulence, wide-angle distortion, and shake can sell a cut without hiding action.

## Build Order

`locked_clips -> careful_splits -> turbulent_effect -> wide_angle_distortion -> transition_shake -> impact_sfx`

## Suggested Rules

- Add transition shake after turbulent and wide-angle effects, not before.
- If Pendulum movement is used before the transition, review the combined swing,
  distortion, and shake together.
- Peak shake on the cut, impact, or beat, usually within 1-2 frames for high-energy edits.
- Settle before the next action pose needs to be read.
- Move or suppress captions over the most chaotic frames.

## QC

- Action remains readable after the shake settles.
- Pendulum plus distortion does not hide the next action pose.
- Wide-angle distortion does not bend the subject or captions beyond recognition.
- SFX lands on the actual transition peak.
