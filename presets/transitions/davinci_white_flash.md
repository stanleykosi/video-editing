# DaVinci White Flash

Use this preset for quick short-form section changes or B-roll entries.

## Suggested Values

- Flash starts `2-3` frames before the cut.
- Peak white opacity on or near the cut.
- Fade down within `2-3` frames after the cut.
- Use the built-in brightness flash only after checking it renders cleanly.

## Rules

- Do not cover captions that must be read.
- Avoid repeated flashes on every cut.
- Pair with a subtle SFX only if the flash needs impact.
