# Typography Contrast Palette

Use this preset when text color, gradient, shadow, or blend mode is the main
readability risk.

## Settings

- Base text: neutral white, black, charcoal, or off-white chosen against the actual
  frame.
- Accent text: one meaning, brand, or comparison color.
- Comparison accents: use a maximum of two opposing accents when the edit needs
  positive/negative or before/after logic.
- Gradients: allowed only on large title or emphasis words with strong edge
  contrast.
- Shadow/stroke: functional only; remove heavy default shadows when contrast already
  works.
- Temperature: match live-action text to the scene's warm/cool frame unless the
  contrast is deliberate.

## Use When

- Text appears over changing backgrounds.
- The style uses gradients, glow, blend/composite modes, or shadow.
- The edit needs clear text hierarchy without adding more motion.

## Avoid When

- Plain high-contrast text already works and extra styling would look cheap.
- The accent colors imply a meaning the story does not support.

## QC

- Text remains readable on the brightest and darkest relevant frames.
- Accent color marks the meaning-changing word or category.
- Gradient, glow, blend mode, or shadow does not soften the text edge.
