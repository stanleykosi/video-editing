# Low-Key Firelight Unification Grade

Use after layer-specific matching when a still-image composite needs a unified
night/firelight look.

## Starting Values

- Exposure/shadows: lower cautiously until the bright source stands out.
- Contrast: raise lightly for night-scene depth.
- Tint/temperature: warm toward the practical light source.
- Highlights: control bright fire/sky frames so they do not clip.
- Glow/filter: subtle only after masks and overlay matching already work.

## Use When

- Fire, sky, reflection, and base still layers need to feel like one shot.

## Avoid When

- Neutral factual color or product accuracy matters more than mood.
- Darkening hides faces, captions, proof, UI, or important architecture.
