# Keyframed Saturation Shift

Use this preset for motivated color-to-muted or color-to-black-and-white changes.

## Pattern

- Normal color keyframe.
- Desaturation keyframe; for a fast black-and-white hit, closer spacing such as
  roughly `6-10 frames` can work when the impact is intentional.
- Hold or restore color when the story beat changes.

## QC

- The shift has a named mood, memory, reveal, or contrast purpose.
- Only saturation moves unless hue, brightness, exposure, or filter strength has
  its own named job.
- Faces, proof, products, brand colors, and captions remain readable.
- Saturation is restored or held intentionally.
