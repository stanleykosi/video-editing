# Fairlight Dialogue Ambience Bus Mix

Use this preset for Resolve scenes that need dialogue clarity plus believable
room tone, music, and layered SFX.

## Track Layout

- Dialogue tracks: split production channels into proper mono tracks when needed.
- Ambience track: low room tone or location air under silent gaps.
- Music track: trimmed to phrase, beat, or matching waveform shape.
- SFX tracks: separate obvious story sounds from layered body/object/environment sounds.
- Buses: route related SFX to a bus for shared EQ/reverb and dialogue to a bus for dynamics.

## Starting Moves

- Lower ambience until it is felt more than noticed.
- Add obvious expected SFX first, then supporting layers.
- Use low-pass/EQ for sounds behind doors, outside rooms, or farther away.
- Use gentle dialogue compression and makeup gain only until speech is clearer.

## QC

- No required track or bus is routed away from the main output.
- Dialogue remains clear without captions rescuing the mix.
- SFX sound balanced in the full mix, not only while soloed.
- Music edits use beats, phrase endings, fades, or crossfades.
