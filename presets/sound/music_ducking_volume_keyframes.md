# Music Ducking Volume Keyframes

Use this preset when one music bed should rise under montage and duck under speech.

## Pattern

- Music full under visual-only section.
- Pre-duck keyframe before speech.
- Low music under the talking section; start around `-15 dB` to `-20 dB` only as
  a guide, then adjust by ear.
- Restore toward full level or `0 dB` after the phrase or speech section ends.

## QC

- Music is licensed, platform-safe, or temp-marked.
- Duck begins before the first spoken word.
- Dialogue is intelligible without captions rescuing the mix.
- The numeric duck value fits this song and voice, not a copied preset.
- Restore does not cover the end of the sentence.
