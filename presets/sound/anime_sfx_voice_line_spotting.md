# Anime SFX Voice Line Spotting

## Use When

- A character/action edit needs hits, whooshes, textures, glitches, risers, or a
  short identity voice line.
- The visuals feel polished but sonically flat.

## Cue Pattern

`locked_visual_hit -> licensed_sfx -> impact_or_texture -> optional_voice_line -> fade_tail -> full_mix_review`

## Suggested Rules

- Place hard hits within 1-2 frames of the visual impact when sync matters.
- Use voice lines only when they clarify identity, emotion, or impact.
- Tag library assets by job: whoosh, hit, impact, texture, glitch, riser,
  transition, voice.
- Match cue families to visible events: eye transformation, lightning entrance,
  glass break, character entrance, and identity voice line should feel distinct.
- Check SFX in the full mix with music and dialogue restored.

## QC

- Each cue has a visible or story job.
- Repeated cues are justified by repeated visible events.
- Voice lines do not clutter every clip.
- Sources are licensed/platform-safe or marked temp-only.
