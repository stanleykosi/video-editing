# SFX Variation Bank

Use this preset to keep common edits from sounding copied and pasted.

## Suggested Library Buckets

- Whooshes
- Hits
- Pops
- Risers
- Ambience
- Meme accents
- Texture beds
- Transition tails

## Rules

- Keep 3-5 usable variants for repeated whooshes or hits.
- Start dense short-form whooshes around -10 dB below dialogue, then adjust by ear.
- Sync energetic hits within 1-2 frames of the motion or impact.
- Use reversed hits only before real questions, reveals, or transitions.
- Lower, shorten, or remove SFX that mask dialogue.
- Avoid default whooshes in serious documentary sections unless the tone explicitly supports them.
