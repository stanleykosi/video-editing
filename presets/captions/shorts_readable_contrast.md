# Shorts Readable Caption Contrast

Use this preset when converting talking-head, podcast, or tutorial clips into vertical shorts.

## Suggested Rules

- Keep captions inside platform safe margins.
- Use stroke, drop shadow, or a backing box when footage changes brightness.
- Avoid covering faces, title text, cards, products, proof visuals, or the action being discussed.
- Remove distracting filler-word caption emphasis when it does not help comprehension.
- For high-energy social edits, one- or two-word caption chunks can work if they stay readable.
- Remove captions where visual text already carries the idea.

## QC

- Preview at phone size.
- Check at least one bright frame and one dark frame.
- Confirm captions do not collide with lower thirds, overlays, or title-safe areas.
- Confirm caption chunks do not cover visual text, icons, proof, or transition objects.
