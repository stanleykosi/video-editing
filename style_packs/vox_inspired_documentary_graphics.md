# Vox-Inspired Documentary Graphics Style Pack

Use this as a style grammar for editorial explainer graphics. Do not copy a
publisher's protected logo, exact layout identity, or unlicensed assets.

## Visual Grammar

- Warm paper or off-white background.
- Low-opacity grid or line texture.
- Charcoal/gray serif display text mixed with small sans labels when useful.
- Yellow, red, and muted accent colors for highlights, markers, and chart bars.
- Hand-drawn route/outline reveals and document highlights.
- Map, chart, screenshot, and cutout scenes grouped as compounds/nests.
- Slight handmade cadence from low-FPS/posterize texture after timing lock.

## Motion Grammar

- Wipe-on arrows, route lines, bars, and highlights.
- Staggered typography and data entrances.
- Eased/cubic map pans, document zooms, and compound scene moves.
- Small rotations only for editorial collage texture.
- Fade or slide exits between graphic sections.

## Sound Grammar

- Light whooshes for major entrances and routes.
- Marker/tick sounds for highlights.
- Shutter-like cues for screenshot or source swaps.
- Restrained piano/classical/editorial music bed under narration.

## QC

- Graphic text and data stay readable after texture and low-FPS effects.
- SFX remain below narration.
- Captions do not cover evidence.
- Assets are licensed, generated, or project-owned and recorded in the manifest.
