# Music Supervision QC

Use this checklist when music choice, licensing, cue timing, or tonal contrast is
central to the edit.

## Track Choice

- The intended tone is written before final track selection.
- At least three plausible tracks are auditioned for important music-led sections when time allows.
- The selected track improves story tone, production value, retention, or emotion.
- The selected track is licensed, platform-safe, or clearly marked temp-only.
- App-library, downloaded, and screen-recorded music/SFX are not assumed safe; the
  source/license/platform status is documented.
- Any rejected but tempting track has a short reject reason.
- If music is added early under voiceover, the voiceover still works without the music.

## Cue Timing

- The main cue or drop lands within 1-2 frames of the intended visual turn in high-energy edits.
- The music cue lands on a story turn, reveal, action change, emotional hit, or section reset.
- Music escalation matches visual escalation.
- The music has a planned duck, filter, or exit before the next story-critical line.
- Beat cuts preserve script logic and do not move visuals away from the spoken idea they support.

## Dialogue And Captions

- Dialogue remains intelligible after music is added.
- Music ducks before important words begin.
- Captions support comprehension but are not used to excuse a bad mix.
- Captions do not cover the visual turn where the music cue lands.
- Final dialogue/music/SFX balance is checked again after an early music-first assembly.

## Diegetic Bridges

- Any radio, speaker, car, room, or phone music source is established or strongly implied.
- The filtered pre-cue sounds intentionally sourced, not broken or muddy.
- The full mix opens on a meaningful visual or story beat.
- The diegetic treatment does not falsely imply music was present in documentary footage.

## Sensitive Contrast

- Beauty-over-horror music creates discomfort, grief, critique, or irony rather than glamour.
- A restrained version is tested for serious, tragic, violent, or crisis material.
- Context remains clear enough that the contrast is not misread as celebration.
- The edit rejects music experiments that are interesting but too far for the story.
