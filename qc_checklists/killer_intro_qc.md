# Killer Intro QC

Use this checklist before approving a formal intro bumper, branded show open, or
premium social-series intro.

## Format Fit

- Intro has a named purpose: brand identity, series identity, social proof, authority, or perceived outcome.
- Intro is used for a suitable format such as podcast, show, social series, creator brand, course, or community packaging.
- Ordinary mid-length YouTube/tutorial content starts with value unless the intro materially increases trust.
- Long-form/show intro stays around 10-15 seconds maximum.
- Social/Reels/Instagram intro stays around 3-5 seconds unless the platform brief says otherwise.

## Brand, Proof, Outcome

- Brand, show name, creator, or series label is readable before motion becomes dense.
- Social proof is truthful, relevant, and preferably comes from someone other than the creator.
- Authority proof is recognizable to the target niche or clearly explained by context.
- Viewer outcome or perceived value is visible or implied before the intro ends.
- Intro body handoff starts before the viewer feels delayed.

## Motion And Text

- Text layers have assigned roles: brand, proof, quote, keyword, underline, or transition.
- One word or phrase has primary hierarchy at each moment.
- Flicker, font changes, motion blur, or 3D camera movement do not reduce readability.
- Captions are visible, repositioned, or explicitly approved for suppression when designed intro text already carries the words.
- Logo animation does not delay proof or outcome.

## Sound

- Every SFX cue lands on a visible event such as logo reveal, text pop, flicker, underline, cut, or transition.
- Intro hits/pops/wooshes land within 0-1 frames when precision matters.
- Music/SFX do not bury proof quotes, spoken intro lines, or the first body line.
- Intro bed fades, ducks, or resolves cleanly into the main content.
- Repeated hits vary or stay low enough that they do not sound mechanical.
