# After Effects Reel Animation QC

Use this checklist before sending an AE-animated Reel back to Premiere or before final export.

## Planning

- Each spoken segment has a visual support decision before animation.
- Required images, icons, overlays, fonts, music, and SFX are organized in the project folder.
- Brand colors, fonts, icon style, and background style are consistent across the Reel.

## After Effects Motion

- Speaker eyes stay aligned during zoom-ins and zoom-outs.
- Original footage and rotoscope/cutout duplicates share the same transform where needed.
- Text, icons, paths, counters, and camera moves are synced to the spoken concept.
- Camera/null layers are trimmed to their intended scene range.
- No camera move reveals empty background edges.
- Track-matte textures keep the original cutout recognizable.
- Path-follow objects travel on the intended visible line.
- Counter final values are readable before the scene changes.
- Elements have out animations or a motivated overlay transition.

## Readability

- Animated text is readable at phone size.
- Glow, shadow, turbulent displacement, grain, grid, and vignette support readability instead of fighting it.
- Captions are visible, repositioned, faded, or explicitly approved for suppression where animation text already carries the line.
- Captions do not cover faces, path endpoints, counters, icons, proof, or CTA words.

## Render And Finish

- Heavy effects disabled during editing are enabled before render.
- AE render is 1080x1920, correct frame rate, and expected duration.
- AE render is watched before Premiere finishing.
- Duplicate render audio is removed or intentionally muted in Premiere.
- Dialogue, music, SFX, and captions are all unmuted/checked before final export.
