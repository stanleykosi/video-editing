# CapCut Graph Keyframe Zoom QC

Use this checklist before approving CapCut graph-shaped keyframes, intense zoom
stacks, or motion-blur zoom effects.

## Graph Shape

- The movement has a named job: emphasis, impact, focus, transition, or style.
- Start speed matches the job: gentle start for smooth focus, fast start for snap
  impact.
- End speed matches the job: gentle settle for readability, sharp stop only for
  intentional impact.
- Linear motion is used only when constant speed is the intended style.
- Copied graph presets are previewed on each clip, not trusted blindly.

## Zoom Safety

- Scale and position are keyframed together so the subject stays centered or
  intentionally framed.
- Heavy zoom values, including 300% first-pass zooms, preserve enough source
  detail for delivery.
- Second-pass zooms, such as 150% on a re-imported clip, add energy without
  making the image too soft.
- Zooms do not crop faces, captions, action, UI, products, or proof.
- Export/re-import passes are used only when the stacked look is worth the
  quality cost.

## Motion Blur

- Motion blur is added after timing and graph curves are locked.
- Blur and blend values are reduced if faces, captions, proof, or action smear.
- The final preview is checked at phone size and delivery resolution.
