# Sensitive Documentary QC

Use this checklist for serious long-form documentary, travel-documentary, crisis,
war, grief, testimony, or human-stakes edits.

## Tone And Opening

- The first minute centers the subject, stakes, evidence, or local voices before creator performance.
- Retention tactics do not make serious material feel manipulative or cheerful.
- The creator perspective arrives after the subject context is clear.
- Any comedy beat functions as relief after heavy context, not as avoidance.

## Pacing And Hold Time

- Dense shots of destruction, maps, signs, documents, or faces are held long enough to study.
- Emotional reactions get a slower test version before the final cut is chosen.
- Long-form sections do not keep fast-cutting out of insecurity after the audience has committed.
- Title cards, if used, arrive after a strong opening and do not delay the initial promise.

## B-Roll Motivation

- Every B-roll shot maps to a story point, contrast, scale cue, detail, or consequence.
- Related shots are grouped into story beats rather than scattered randomly.
- Repeated shots are intentional and not caused by a rushed edit.
- Wide-to-close pairs are used when the story needs both scale and human detail.
- Maps and route graphics stay onscreen until the route or distance is understandable.

## Movement And Focal Point

- Smooth B-roll strings use compatible movement direction unless contrast is intentional.
- Each shot has a clear focal point before the cut.
- Any flipped shot is checked for reversed text, false geography, or misleading direction.
- Location entrances feel like arrival, not random coverage.

## Audio And Captions

- Every story-critical line is intelligible to a first-time viewer.
- Hard-to-hear lines are repaired, subtitled, or cut.
- Music and SFX duck under testimony and important dialogue.
- Subtitles take priority over lower thirds when both cannot coexist.
- Related B-roll uses a consistent ambience rule.

## Graphics, Music, And Transitions

- Graphics and lower thirds match the maturity of the subject.
- Screen wipes, goofy effects, and old channel templates are removed unless intentionally justified.
- Music is licensed or platform-safe and tonally appropriate.
- Stock music does not make serious material feel generic or disrespectful.
- Match cuts have explicit story meaning and do not imply false relationships.

## Ethics

- Reordered documentary lines or events preserve factual truth and speaker intent.
- Sensitive reorders are traceable to source timestamps and flagged for human review.
- The edit does not imply causality, chronology, or relationships that did not exist.
- Subject dignity is preserved in crop, shot choice, captions, graphics, and music.
