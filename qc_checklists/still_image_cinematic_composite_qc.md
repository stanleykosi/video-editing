# Still Image Cinematic Composite QC

Use this checklist before approving a still-image-to-moving-scene composite.

## Asset Fit

- Every moving overlay has a named role: fire, sky, smoke, reflection, ambience,
  texture, or mood.
- Overlay perspective, scale, brightness, and motion direction match the base
  still image.
- External stock, generated, or library assets have source/license notes before
  publishable delivery.

## Blend And Mask

- Screen/Add overlays remove black backgrounds cleanly.
- Sky, atmosphere, fire, glow, and reflection masks have no visible hard edge.
- Feathered masks are checked at full playback speed, not only on a paused frame.
- Moving overlays do not cover faces, captions, products, UI, proof, or key
  architecture.

## Local Light

- Flicker or strobe affects only surfaces close enough to the visible light
  source.
- Reflected light is weaker than or consistent with the source light.
- Flicker does not continue after the visible or implied light source clears.
- Captions do not sit on top of contrast-changing flicker regions.

## Grade

- Base image shadows are dark enough for mood but not crushed where detail is
  needed.
- Fire, sky, reflection, and base layers match in exposure, temperature, and
  contrast.
- Warm/glow filters do not turn neutral surfaces orange or create muddy
  compression.
- Captions and important subjects remain readable on the brightest and darkest
  frames.
