# Premiere Reels Workflow QC

Use this checklist before exporting a Premiere-built Reel, Short, or TikTok-style edit.

## Rough Cut

- Silences, bad takes, and accidental opening hesitation are removed.
- J-cuts or audio smoothing are used where hard dialogue cuts feel harsh.
- Rough cut is understandable before captions, graphics, zooms, or music are enabled.
- External mic audio is synced and unwanted camera/source audio is removed or muted intentionally.
- Prepared vertical render is 1080x1920, synced, trimmed, corrected, and watched before AE animation begins.
- Voiceover-led edits have VO trimmed for flow before music or beat cuts shape the timeline.
- Script beats are tagged by visual need before assets are layered.

## Captions

- Auto-caption wording, timing, chunking, and placement are corrected before graphic conversion.
- One-word captions are readable at phone size and do not cover faces, hands, products, proof, or platform UI.
- Pop-fade caption motion finishes before the spoken word needs to be read.
- Duplicate captions remain visible, repositioned, simplified, or explicitly approved for suppression where hook text, B-roll text, or full-screen text carries the same idea.
- Captions are visible, repositioned, faded, or explicitly approved for suppression over dense AE animation, counters, paths, icons, or CTA text that already carries the line.
- Center/emphasis text is used only for important ideas that captions cannot carry cleanly.
- Text pop presets settle before the phrase needs to be read.

## Hook And Body Visuals

- Hook text design is readable within the first five seconds and has one clear keyword or phrase priority.
- Filler background text cards are used only for phrases that do not need proof footage.
- B-roll text sequences match the spoken story beat and preserve visible proof or action after dim/desaturation.
- Full-screen object animations are reserved for important concepts, transitions, or payoff lines.
- Each segment has a chosen visual support type before After Effects work begins.
- Brand colors, fonts, icon style, backgrounds, and overlay style remain consistent across the Reel.
- Simple animations have sketches/plans and are exported sharp enough for later zooms.
- Screen-blend overlays are trimmed to specific highlight or transition beats and do not cover proof or captions.

## Post Motion And Sound

- Adjustment-layer zooms affect only the intended section range.
- Zoom and blur settle before the viewer needs to read captions, text, UI, product, or proof.
- Music was auditioned for tone fit and does not bury speech.
- Track license or platform safety is documented, or the track is marked temp-only.
- AE render is aligned to the Premiere timeline and watched before final finishing.
- Duplicate audio from the AE render is removed, muted, or intentionally used.
- Whooshes, hits, typewriter cues, and reward sounds land on visible motion frames.
- Dialogue, music, SFX, captions, and overlay tracks are unmuted/visible before export.
- Export range, sequence, filename, resolution, and platform target are checked after any AE roundtrip.
- Early music choices are rechecked after visuals and SFX are complete.
- Isolated SFX spotting is followed by a full-mix review with dialogue and music restored.
