# CapCut Pro Compositing QC

Use this checklist before approving CapCut subject sandwiches, chroma-key text,
tracking stickers, photo stacks, PiP borders, split-mask illusions, clone effects,
or green-screen VFX.

## Layer Order

- Behind-subject effects use the intended stack: prepared base, behind element or
  VFX, foreground subject cutout.
- Direct text-behind-object effects use the intended stack: base video, text
  layer, foreground cutout duplicate.
- Prepared base and foreground clean clip are frame-aligned.
- Export/re-import passes are used only after baked text or VFX timing is stable.
- Captions and final SFX remain editable at the top level unless delivery is
  already locked.

## Cutout, Key, And Mask

- Remove Background edges stay clean around hair, hands, fast turns, and motion
  blur.
- Chroma key removes the matte color without specks, spill, or erased VFX/video
  detail.
- Video-in-text and outline reveal mattes preserve crisp letter edges without
  muddy blend modes or colored specks.
- Foreground-lift text masks remain clean while the text moves from hidden to
  readable.
- Split masks align to believable object edges and do not reveal brightness or
  focus mismatch.
- PiP borders align evenly with the masked video shape.

## Tracking And Overlays

- Tracked faces, bodies, hands, stickers, or arrows do not drift, jitter, or lose
  the target.
- Slide-on photos and chapter cards have a clear reason to appear and clear
  before the next focal beat.
- Overlays avoid captions, faces, hands, UI, products, proof, and active action.
- Outline reveal, perspective text, and video-in-text effects have one clear
  reading path and do not cover the subject detail they are meant to enhance.
- Highlight, gradient, and glow text layers are aligned to their duplicate base
  text and do not create muddy letter edges.
- Photo, sticker, and green-screen assets have source/license notes.

## Capture And Sound

- Split-mask and clone footage was captured with locked exposure, focus, white
  balance, and camera position.
- Clone dialogue/reactions are timed naturally and do not overlap by accident.
- VFX hits, footsteps, door sounds, and sticker pops land on visible events and
  stay below speech.
- Text flicker, font-shift, and counter ticks end with the visual animation and
  stay below speech.
- Glitch, shimmer, or glow-reveal cues end with the visible text effect and stay
  below speech.
- Music and SFX are licensed, platform-safe, or marked temp-only.
