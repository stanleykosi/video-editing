# Viral YouTube Workflow QC

Use this checklist before exporting or publishing a scripted viral-style YouTube edit.

## Idea And Script

- Target audience is written before edit style is chosen.
- Idea passes a stranger-click test for that target audience.
- Script has a clear main promise and progress structure.
- Any secondary story layer supports the main promise instead of delaying it.
- Useless lines, bad jokes, repeated points, and dragged sections are removed.
- Meaningful pauses are kept only when they add tension, humor, emotion, or comprehension.

## Asset And Timeline Prep

- Script beats are tagged by visual need: screen record, image, video, text, overlay, animation, SFX, or no added visual.
- Required assets are gathered and organized before the heavy timeline pass.
- Voiceover is trimmed for flow before music or effects drive timing.
- Music is licensed/platform-safe or clearly marked temp-only.
- Beat cuts land on meaningful story or visual moments, not random beats.

## Motion And Text

- Animations have sketches or written plans before build.
- Simple animations are exported sharp enough for any later zoom.
- Focus points stay stable across zooms and cuts using guide, eyeline, center, or rule-of-thirds targets.
- Text pop, center text, and scene-attached text are used only for important ideas.
- Text pop overshoot settles before the viewer needs to read the phrase.
- Screen-blend overlays are trimmed to specific highlight or transition beats.
- Overlays do not hide captions, faces, UI, products, or proof.

## Sound, Color, And Publish

- Isolated SFX pass is followed by a full-mix review with dialogue and music restored.
- SFX peaks stay below dialogue and have clean fades or cuts.
- Basic color correction preserves faces, proof, and text readability.
- Final output has no missing media, muted required tracks, caption collisions, black frames, or accidental overlays.
- Remaining improvements are optional, not blocking.
- One new technique or lesson is logged for the next project.
- Publish or hold decision is explicit.
