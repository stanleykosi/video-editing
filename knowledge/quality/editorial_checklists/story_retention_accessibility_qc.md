# Story, Retention, And Accessibility QC

Use this checklist for edits where montage structure, action clarity, viewer eye
path, music choice, or audience accessibility affect the result.

## Montage Intent

- Vague sections like "montage" have been converted into beat-level intent.
- Every montage shot serves a written beat or deliberately replaces one.
- Montage beats progress instead of repeating the same idea.
- Sound, captions, motion, and color support the beat plan.

## Character Action

- The action sequence is anchored to a character perspective, stake, or reaction.
- Dialogue and reactions are not buried under spectacle.
- Action geography remains readable after speed ramps, shake, grade, and captions.
- The sequence returns to character impact after the peak.

## Narration Visual Alignment

- Every named character, person, place, object, product, UI element, proof point, or event in narration has a matching visual in the rendered output.
- Important named-entity handoffs start slightly before the viewer-facing timestamp so rounded player times do not show the previous subject.
- Timestamped contact sheets from the rendered preview or final have been reviewed around each sensitive handoff.
- User-reported timestamp problems are inspected with context: at least 0.5s before through 1.0s after the reported window.
- The promoted final file passes the same spot check; source sheets and EDL math alone are not enough.

## Eye Trace

- The viewer can find key information immediately after smoothness-critical cuts.
- Captions and overlays do not pull attention away from the next focal point.
- Reframes, crops, or motion guide the eye when the next focal point moves.
- Cuts have been reviewed at phone size or a reduced preview scale.

## Music And Licensing

- Music is licensed or platform-safe for the intended release.
- Needle drops land on meaningful reveals, section shifts, or emotional payoffs.
- Music does not bury speech or important source audio.
- Music exits, ducks, or transitions cleanly before the next story beat.

## Artful Versus Practical

- The target audience is defined before judging artful choices.
- Broad-audience edits state the premise quickly and clearly.
- Niche or art-first edits preserve the intended authored experience.
- Artful flourishes do not hide the subject, proof, product, or emotional information.
- The edit keeps enough personality to avoid feeling purely optimized.

## Viral Workflow

- Target audience and style lane are written before polish begins.
- Idea and script pass a stranger-click/care test for the target audience.
- Any secondary story layer supports the main promise and has visible progress.
- Meaningful pauses are kept only when they add tension, humor, emotion, or comprehension.
- Optional improvements are separated from publish-blocking issues.

## Slow Human Retention

- Slow sections have a named connection, proof, emotion, or comprehension purpose.
- Kept imperfections create warmth, vulnerability, humor, or character.
- Any unscripted human moment has no consent, privacy, dignity, or brand risk.
- First 3-5 seconds still create a reason to continue before the edit slows down.
- Warm grade, restrained captions, and foley support the human moment without overpowering it.

## Editorial Documentary Graphics

- Map routes, chart values, source documents, and screenshots are tied to the current claim.
- Maps, charts, and highlights remain readable after paper texture, warmth, blend modes, and low-FPS effects.
- Source screenshots and document highlights show enough context to avoid misleading cherry-picking.
- Captions and labels do not cover the evidence they are meant to explain.
- The style is project-specific and does not copy protected publisher identity or unlicensed logos.

## Video Typography

- Font family matches the story tone, industry lane, and reference without reducing readability.
- Each title, caption, label, or CTA has one clear primary word or phrase.
- Line breaks form readable phrases and do not isolate filler words as emphasis.
- Tracking, kerning, line spacing, gradients, shadows, and blend modes remain readable at final size.
- Live-action text sits in negative space or tracks cleanly without covering faces, products, proof, or emotional reactions.
- For ads, the product/result/offer/CTA reads faster than the typography style is noticed.
