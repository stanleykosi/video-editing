# CapCut AMV Editing Tricks QC

Use this checklist for high-energy CapCut, anime-style, action, sports, or
music-led montage edits that use speed matching, dense overlays, masks, turbulent
transitions, wide-angle distortion, SFX, and voice lines.

## Timing

- Beat markers exist before multiple clips are speed-matched.
- Each action peak lands within the intended beat tolerance, usually 1-2 frames
  for high-energy sections.
- Speed changes preserve action readability and leave handles for transitions.
- Transition shakes are tuned after turbulent and wide-angle effects are visible.
- Effect-tail extensions clear before the next main beat.

## Motion And Effects

- Character masks, cutouts, glow, shadow, and flicker preserve clean edges.
- Glow or shadow emphasizes the intended body part or subject, not the whole frame
  by accident.
- Turbulent and wide-angle effects do not bend faces, action, captions, products,
  or proof beyond recognition.
- Pre-rendered or replaced clips match the intended duration and beat alignment.
- Temporary black screens, spacers, or placeholders used during replacement are
  removed or replaced before final export.
- Duplicated effect layers do not create muddy frames, banding, or compression dirt.
- Eye/power overlays stay aligned through playback, not just on one paused frame.
- Pendulum movement is checked with the final transition stack and does not hide
  the next readable pose.

## Sound

- Every SFX cue has a visible motion, impact, reveal, transition, or character job.
- Cue families match visible events, such as eye transformation, lightning,
  glass break, entrance, or identity voice line.
- Hard hits, whooshes, and transition cues land within the intended sync tolerance.
- Voice lines clarify identity or emotion instead of cluttering every clip.
- SFX tails are faded or trimmed before they mask the next clip.
- Music, SFX, and voice lines are licensed/platform-safe or marked temp-only.

## Captions And Readability

- Captions do not cover the action pose, character glow, transition peak, or
  important subject edge.
- Captions are moved, restyled, or explicitly approved for suppression during the most chaotic transition frames.
- Action remains readable at phone size after speed, masks, glow, flicker, and shake.
- Face/eye transformation beats remain readable and caption-safe at phone size.
- The final preview is watched with all overlays, music, SFX, and voice lines active.
