# DaVinci Short-Form Workflow QC

Use this checklist before exporting a DaVinci Resolve short-form edit.

## Project And Timeline

- Project is set to vertical resolution before caption and overlay layout.
- Original, shorter, and balanced timeline versions are named when major pacing versions were tested.
- Export comes from the intended named timeline/version.
- A-roll cuts have no accidental dead air longer than 300 ms.
- B-roll appears on key lines rather than random filler.
- Unwanted B-roll source audio is muted or deleted.
- Effects Library and Inspector-dependent changes have been reviewed in the rendered preview, not only the timeline.

## Fusion Motion

- Fusion Transform animations use eased Spline curves instead of unintended linear movement.
- Strong zooms preserve face, hands, captions, proof, and action.
- Dynamic Zoom or manual Transform moves land on intentional beats.
- Dynamic Zoom used for a slow human hook or body section preserves face, proof, and captions.
- Adjustment clips do not affect unintended clips outside their target range.

## Text And Captions

- Auto captions are styled, positioned, and readable at phone size.
- Text+ premium captions are used only on emphasis moments.
- Text+ stacks do not duplicate auto captions unless the duplicate is intentional.
- Gradient, glow, blur, glitch, or Stop Motion effects preserve legibility.
- Captions and Text+ elements avoid face, hands, proof, and title-safe areas.

## Transitions And Overlays

- White flashes are brief and do not hide readable captions or key action.
- Built-in flash transitions render cleanly; manual flashes have correct opacity keyframes.
- Overlays using Add/blend-style treatment are rotated, scaled, and checked for unintended edges.
- Recycled animations still fit the new beat and crop.

## Presets And Efficiency

- Any saved Power Bin preset has been phone-size checked before reuse.
- Power Bin preset names describe their job and motion.
- Shortcut-based moves did not desync captions, SFX, or B-roll.
- If viewer background was changed to inspect black edges, final viewer/export background assumptions are restored.
- In-app voiceover recordings are checked for level, noise, and timing.
- Slow human sections have a named connection, proof, emotion, or comprehension purpose before final render.
