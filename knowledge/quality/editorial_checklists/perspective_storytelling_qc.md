# Perspective Storytelling QC

Use this checklist for character-driven challenge videos, documentaries, learning
arcs, creator stories, and any edit that depends on protagonist perspective.

## Perspective

- Each major story turn answers what the new information means to the protagonist.
- Reaction shots are placed at moments of changed stakes, status, confidence, or emotion.
- The edit does not rely only on narration to explain what the protagonist feels.
- Captions, graphics, and crops do not cover the protagonist reaction.

## Conflict And Transformation

- The protagonist starts with a clear goal, belief, or perceived ability.
- An obstacle or humbling beat appears early enough to create conflict.
- The learning phase contains visible attempts, feedback, or changed behavior.
- The payoff feels earned by the journey.

## Open Loops

- The opening confirms the promised goal or action.
- The opening leaves a truthful gap or near-miss instead of fully satisfying the promise.
- Every opened loop has a later closure or clear reframing.
- The gap is real, visible, and not manufactured.

## Expert And Pickup Inserts

- Experts are introduced with proof of skill before their advice matters.
- Expert reactions or feedback visibly change protagonist confidence or direction.
- Pickup inserts solve a specific story gap.
- Pickup inserts preserve truth, continuity, color, and sound texture.

## Visual And Sound Readability

- Small-object action is center-frame or deliberately guided.
- Repetition for scale stops once the pattern is clear.
- Music tone matches the character state or narrative phase.
- Dialogue and natural sounds remain intelligible under music.
