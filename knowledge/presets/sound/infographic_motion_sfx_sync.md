# Infographic Motion SFX Sync

Use this pass after a graphic sequence's motion timing is locked.

## Cue Types

- Whoosh: major word, cutout, map, or scene entrance.
- Tick/marker: route point, highlight, chart emphasis.
- Shutter/camera: screenshot or source swap.
- Soft hit: final title or section arrival.

## Starting Values

- Sync cues within `1-2 frames` of the visible reveal.
- Start around `-12 dB` below narration and adjust by ear.
- Avoid SFX on every tiny layer.
- Crossfade music exits at natural phrase endings.

## QC

- Every SFX cue maps to a visible event.
- SFX stay below narration.
- Documentary tone remains credible.
