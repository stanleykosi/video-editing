# Isolated SFX Design Pass

Use when a visually active edit feels sonically flat and needs a focused SFX pass.

## Pass Order

1. Lock or nearly lock visible motion.
2. Temporarily mute dialogue and music for SFX spotting.
3. Add whooshes, risers, impacts, clicks, texture, or context sounds.
4. Restore dialogue and music.
5. Lower SFX until speech stays dominant.
6. Add short fades or clean cuts on SFX edges.

## Use When

- Motion, text, overlays, or transitions need sound support.

## Avoid When

- Dialogue timing is still changing.
- The tone needs sparse natural sound.
- The full-mix review cannot be completed.
