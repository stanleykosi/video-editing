# Reverse Hit And Whoosh Motion Sync

Use this preset for high-energy short-form text pops, transitions, zooms, and reveal beats.

## Suggested Values

- Whoosh level: start around -10 dB below dialogue, then adjust by ear.
- Hit sync: within 1-2 frames of the visual impact.
- Reverse hit: place before a question, reveal, or transition to build anticipation.
- Fade edges: add tiny fades to avoid clicks.

## Rules

- Match every whoosh or hit to actual movement, text pop, or reveal.
- Lower or remove SFX when it masks speech.
- Vary repeated whooshes and hits.
- Keep background music low enough that speech stays dominant.
