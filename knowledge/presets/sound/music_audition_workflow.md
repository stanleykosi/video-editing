# Music Audition Workflow Preset Suggestions

Source lesson: `Saving Bad Videos With Only Music`

## Use When

- A sequence feels cheap, generic, flat, or tonally wrong even though the footage or concept is usable.
- Music will carry retention, production value, genre, intimacy, comedy, or emotional contrast.
- A social short needs a platform-native track that fits the finished edit's actual tone.

## Candidate Metadata

- `intended_tone`: intimate, premium, comic, stylish, haunting, bitter, energetic, restrained.
- `license_status`: licensed, platform-safe, temp-only, unsafe.
- `cue_point`: first usable musical cue.
- `drop_frame`: frame where the main visual turn should land.
- `dialogue_risk`: low, medium, high.
- `reject_reason`: short reason if the track is not chosen.

## Audition Pass

- Pick 3-5 tracks that could plausibly fit the intended tone.
- Align each track's strongest cue to the same story or visual turn.
- Render short A/B tests at delivery volume.
- Choose the track that best serves story tone and dialogue clarity, not the most famous track.
- For Reels/Shorts, test tracks under the finished body edit and reject tracks that make the emotion wrong even if they are trendy.

## QC

- Track is licensed or marked temp-only.
- Dialogue remains intelligible.
- Cue lands on a meaningful story or visual beat.
- Interesting-but-wrong tracks are rejected with notes.
- Platform safety is documented before final export.
