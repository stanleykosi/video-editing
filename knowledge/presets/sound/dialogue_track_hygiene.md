# Dialogue Track Hygiene

Use this preset before final export on YouTube, shorts, podcast, or tutorial edits.

## Track Layout

- Dialogue or main speaker on dedicated track/group.
- Music on dedicated track/group.
- SFX and ambience on separate tracks/groups where possible.
- Temporary or reference audio clearly labeled.

## Suggested Mix Rules

- Keep the full mix out of red and leave practical headroom around -3 dBFS before platform loudness checks.
- Duck music before important words start.
- Use clip gain or keyframes for quiet words instead of raising the entire clip.
- Fade SFX and music edges to avoid clicks.
