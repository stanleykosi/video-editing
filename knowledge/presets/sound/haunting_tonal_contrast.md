# Haunting Tonal Contrast Preset Suggestions

Source lesson: `Saving Bad Videos With Only Music`

## Use When

- Serious imagery needs discomfort, grief, critique, or historical irony.
- Straight grim music feels too obvious, but upbeat scoring would be disrespectful.

## Track Traits

- Beautiful but restrained.
- Nostalgic, fragile, elegiac, or bitter rather than triumphant.
- Strong enough to create contrast but not so catchy that it turns harm into spectacle.

## Review Pass

- Compare against a restrained version.
- Check whether the track sharpens the horror or glamorizes it.
- Preserve narration, captions, or context needed to frame the contrast.

## QC

- The contrast does not celebrate harm.
- The edit preserves subject dignity.
- The story owner approves the stylized direction for sensitive material.
