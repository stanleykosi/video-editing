# Frame-Locked Intro SFX And Drums

Use this preset for premium intros with fast logo, text, flicker, underline, or
camera movement.

## Starting Pattern

- Music bed: fast pulse or drum pattern, often near 120 BPM if it fits the brand.
- Logo reveal: one short hit or sub hit.
- Text/flicker beats: small pops, ticks, or short glitch accents.
- Keyword/underline: one stronger but still speech-safe accent.
- Handoff: 4-12 frame duck/fade when body speech starts immediately.

## QC

- Every cue has a visible event.
- Precision hits land within 0-1 frames of the event.
- Proof quote and body speech stay intelligible.
- Repeated hits are varied or mixed low enough to avoid a pasted-template feel.
