# Sensory Worldbuilding Sound Preset Suggestions

Source lesson: `The 5-Second Intro Is Dead: Here's Why`

## Use When

- A long-form documentary intro needs the place, material, or unreal world to feel alive before VO explains it.
- A pause beat should let image plus sound carry meaning.
- A ramped sequence needs a grounded silence reset.

## Suggested Layers

- `world_bed`: low tonal swell or restrained drone for scale and hidden tension.
- `material_texture`: quiet sand, ground, machinery, room tone, wind, or surface detail.
- `conflict_impact`: heavier impact, rumble, or destructive layer for opposing force.
- `unreal_layer`: distorted organic or synthetic texture for fantasy, CGI, simulation, or promotional-world shots.
- `grounded_reset`: natural footsteps, camera handling, room tone, or location ambience after abrupt silence.

## Timing Defaults

- Pause beat: 1-2 seconds when the image is readable and the sound has a clear story job.
- Duck point: lower SFX/music before VO re-entry, not after the first word.
- Silence reset: place after a ramp or dense barrage, then land on grounded natural sound.

## QC

- Every layer has a named story job.
- SFX peaks do not mask VO or testimony.
- Abrupt silence feels intentional, not like an audio dropout.
- Generic whooshes are removed when they do not fit the documentary tone.
