# Diegetic Radio Filter Bridge Preset Suggestions

Source lesson: `Saving Bad Videos With Only Music`

## Use When

- A track should begin as if it comes from a car, phone, room, venue, radio, or speaker.
- The music needs motivation before expanding into the full mix.

## Starting Filter

- Narrow bandwidth: reduce low bass and high sparkle.
- Lower gain than the full mix.
- Narrow stereo image or collapse toward mono.
- Add light room/source texture if available.

## Transition

- Establish or imply the source before the filter starts.
- Remove the filter and raise level on an action turn, reveal, wide shot, or section shift.
- Duck before dialogue returns.

## QC

- Source is clear or strongly implied.
- Filtered audio sounds intentional, not broken.
- Full mix opens on a meaningful beat.
- Documentary usage remains truthful.
