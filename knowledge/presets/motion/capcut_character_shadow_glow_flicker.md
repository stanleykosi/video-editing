# CapCut Character Shadow Glow Flicker

## Use When

- A character or object needs isolated glow, shadow, or flicker emphasis.
- The edit is stylized, music-led, and action-focused.

## Layer Pattern

`base_clip -> duplicate_or_cutout -> subject_mask -> shadow_or_glow -> short_flicker -> edge_review`

## Suggested Settings

- Keep glow on the subject detail that carries the beat, such as eyes, hair,
  weapon edge, or silhouette.
- Use short opacity or intensity bursts around the impact frame instead of a
  constant flicker.
- Hold the hero pose briefly after the glow lands so the viewer can read it.
- Review mask edges at full speed and phone size.

## QC

- Mask edges stay clean across motion.
- Glow does not cover captions or action.
- Source footage and cutouts have rights notes for real projects.
