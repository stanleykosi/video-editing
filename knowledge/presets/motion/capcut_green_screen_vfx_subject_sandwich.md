# CapCut Green-Screen VFX Subject Sandwich

Use when a keyed VFX element should sit behind a person in a CapCut shot.

## Starting Values

- Middle layer: green-screen VFX, cropped and chroma-keyed.
- Bottom layer: original base clip.
- Top layer: duplicated original clip with Remove Background.
- Match: reduce brightness or grade VFX if it looks pasted on.
- Sound: place VFX hit after impact frame is locked.

## Avoid When

- Asset rights are unclear or the key leaves green spill/holes.
