# Remotion Staggered Spring Text

Use this preset for word-lift, smooth caption groups, elastic pop, or short hook
phrases that should build with polish.

## Pattern

`phrase split -> per-word delay -> spring transform/opacity -> readable hold`

## Settings

- Large title delay: around 8 frames between words or lines.
- Character pop delay: around 3 frames for short elastic title words.
- Lift distance: around 120 px for large titles; reduce for captions.
- Elastic start: damping around 8 and stiffness around 140 only when playful
  overshoot fits the tone.

## QC

- Final word lands before the next visual claim.
- Overshoot does not crop letters or collide with safe areas.
- Caption line breaks and impact words were corrected before animation.
