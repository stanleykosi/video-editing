# Reality/Fantasy Visual Treatment Preset Suggestions

Source lesson: `The 5-Second Intro Is Dead: Here's Why`

## Use When

- A documentary needs to distinguish grounded reality, promotional fantasy, proof, or a character's imagined future.
- A sequence needs repeated visual grammar rather than one-off style effects.

## Treatment Slots

- `reality`: no letterbox unless the whole project uses it; preserve natural movement and grounded sound.
- `fantasy`: optional letterbox/aspect treatment, subtle rotation motif, grouped movement direction, and unreal sound layer.
- `proof`: prioritize readability; avoid heavy treatment that makes evidence look fake or decorative.
- `exception`: break the fantasy/reality grammar only when the story perspective explains the break.

## Motion Defaults

- Tracking back: use to reveal scale or pull the viewer out of a fantasy.
- Tracking forward: use to invite the viewer into a world.
- Rotation motif: keep subtle and consistent; inspect crop edges for black borders.
- Digital zoom-out against forward camera movement: use sparingly for unstable or unreal parallax.

## QC

- The editor can explain what each treatment means.
- Treatment exceptions are intentional and tied to perspective, belief, or proof.
- Rotation, zoom, and aspect changes preserve maps, evidence, faces, and body language.
- Captions do not collide with letterbox bars or proof screenshots.
