# After Effects Text Animation Stack

Use this preset family for high-quality Reel text that needs reusable motion and texture.

## Suggested Values

- Smooth typewriter: Text Animator with ramp-up shape and Offset -100 to 100.
- Position+opacity entrance: opacity starts slightly before position settles.
- Turbulent Displace: Amount 10-15, Size 6-8 as a starting point.
- Random Seed motion: expression such as `time*8` when subtle living texture is needed.
- Highlight line: Trim Paths End 0 to 100 with eased keyframes.

## Rules

- Center anchor points before scaling or saving presets.
- Save presets by behavior, such as `smooth_typewriter`, `slide_up`, `glow_shadow`, or `basic_turbulence`.
- Disable heavy effects only while editing; enable them before render.
- Do not animate text that duplicates captions unless the duplicate is intentional emphasis.
