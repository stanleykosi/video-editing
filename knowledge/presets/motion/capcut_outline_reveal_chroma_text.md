# CapCut Outline Reveal Chroma Text

Use this preset for filled and outlined text that reveals with foreground subject
depth.

## Starting Values

- Duplicate the video.
- Top video: Remove Background or Custom Removal.
- Text: filled layer plus outline layer.
- Stroke: around `5` for cleaner white outline text, or around `8` for larger
  bold versions, then adjust by font size.
- Cutout: Feather `5` and Expansion `5` can be tested, then tuned.
- Chroma: use key color as a matte only, then remove it cleanly.

## QC

- Chroma key leaves no green/color specks.
- Foreground cutout stays clean through hair, hands, and motion blur.
- Filled and outline layers have clear roles.
- Captions, faces, proof, UI, products, and action remain safe.
