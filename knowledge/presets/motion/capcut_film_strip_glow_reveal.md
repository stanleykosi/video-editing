# CapCut Film Strip Glow Reveal

Use this preset for short title text that needs a controlled commercial-style
shine on a dark or restrained background.

## Starting Values

- Base text: darker project color that is already readable.
- Duplicate text: white, compounded.
- Mask: `Film Strip`.
- Mask rotation: around `60` degrees.
- Glow: `Glow 2`, Size around `46`, Intensity around `20`.
- Start/end: keep the mask fully off the word before and after the sweep.

## QC

- Glow does not appear before the shine reaches the word.
- Glow exits fully after the sweep.
- Letter edges stay crisp at phone size.
- Captions, faces, product UI, proof, and action remain safe.
