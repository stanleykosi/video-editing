# CapCut Axis Stretch Word

Use this preset when a single word needs vertical or horizontal emphasis.

## Pattern

- Select the designed text layer.
- Disable Uniform Scale.
- Keyframe Height for vertical stretch or Width for horizontal stretch.
- Move forward, increase only the chosen axis, then hold or restore.

## QC

- The stretched word is the impact word.
- The word remains readable at maximum stretch.
- The effect is not applied to continuous subtitles.
- Text avoids faces, proof, UI, products, and captions.
