# CapCut Video-In-Text Chroma Portal

Use for a title or opener where footage appears inside bold text and the edit
zooms through the letters into the scene.

## Starting Values

- Text: one short word or a few large letters.
- Font: very bold, thick letterforms.
- Fast method: use Video > Mask > Text Mask when no text animation is needed.
- Matte: colored text over black, then export as a prepared clip.
- Key: Chroma Key the text color over destination footage.
- Animated method: compound black background plus editable text, blend fill video
  into the text, compound again, set wrapper to Screen, then animate the internal text.
- Motion: keyframe a zoom through a large readable letter.

## Avoid When

- The phrase is long, the font is thin, blend mode muddies the footage, or chroma
  key leaves colored edge specks.
