# CapCut Perspective Freeze Text

Use this preset for angled title words that fit a scene or thumbnail-style layout.

## Pattern

- Create text and compound it.
- Apply a perspective-style animation such as `Flip 6`.
- Choose the most readable angled frame.
- If the animation shrinks the text, reopen the compound and resize the internal
  text instead of scaling a degraded result.
- Compound again and freeze frame.
- Delete unused animated tail sections.
- Repeat for each word, then stagger and group reposition/scale.
- For landscapes, rotate and scale each frozen word to match slopes, walls, or
  distant planes.

## QC

- Each frozen word reads at phone size.
- Words fit scene geometry or intentional negative space.
- Blend mode preserves letter edges.
- The stagger has one clear reading order.
- Scene-attached words match the angle, scale, and distance of the plane they sit on.
