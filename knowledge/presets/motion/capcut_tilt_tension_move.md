# CapCut Tilt Tension Move

Use this preset for a quick eerie, unstable, comedic, or disorienting frame tilt.

## Pattern

- Add starting keyframe.
- Zoom in enough to hide rotation corners.
- Add rotation keyframe.
- Hold, reset, or cut intentionally.

## QC

- Tilt has a named tone or story job.
- No black corners appear.
- Captions, faces, proof, UI, and action remain visible.
