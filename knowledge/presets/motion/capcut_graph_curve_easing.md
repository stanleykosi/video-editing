# CapCut Graph Curve Easing

## Use When

- A keyframed CapCut move feels linear, robotic, rough, or too abrupt.
- Scale, position, rotation, opacity, color, or volume needs a more intentional
  start and end speed.

## Curve Rules

- Low left side: slower start.
- High left side: faster start.
- Low right side: gentler ending.
- High right side: sharper stop.
- Upward curve: acceleration.
- Downward curve: deceleration.
- Straight middle line: constant speed.

## QC

- The graph shape matches the movement's job.
- The subject remains readable through the curve.
- Captions, faces, UI, proof, products, and action stay safe.
