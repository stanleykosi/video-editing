# Premiere Text Pop Scale 70-110-100

Use for center/emphasis text that needs a quick energetic pop without a full After Effects pass.

## Starting Values

- Keyframe 1: Scale `70%`.
- Keyframe 2: about `7 frames` later, Scale `110%`.
- Keyframe 3: about `5 frames` later, Scale `100%`.
- Ease keyframes only if readability remains crisp.

## Use When

- A key word or short phrase needs emphasis beyond ordinary captions.
- The text does not cover faces, proof, products, UI, or captions.

## Avoid When

- The line is normal subtitle content.
- The bounce feels wrong for the subject tone.
- The phrase is too long to read during the pop.
