# CapCut Eye Power Overlay Alignment

## Use When

- A character eye, symbol, or power detail needs a precise transformation beat.
- The source moment needs a short speed adjustment before overlay alignment.

## Build Pattern

`beat_marker -> speed_adjust -> overlay_eye_asset -> mask/scale/position -> pre-render -> replace -> SFX cue`

## Suggested Rules

- Align the overlay against playback, not only a paused frame.
- Keep the effect short enough that tracking drift does not become visible.
- Export/re-import only after duration and beat alignment are stable.
- Keep captions away from the face or power detail.

## QC

- Overlay reveal lands within the intended beat tolerance.
- Eye, face, and expression remain readable at phone size.
- Overlay asset has source/license status before publishable use.
