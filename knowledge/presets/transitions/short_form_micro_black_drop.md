# Short-Form Micro Black Drop

Use this transition as a rhythm reset after a human pattern interrupt, intense
build, reveal, or emotional turn.

## Pattern

- Build intensity with tighter cuts, motion, ambience, riser, or whoosh.
- Insert a very brief black/drop gap or open-space pause.
- Re-enter with calmer density or the next story section.

## Starting Values

- Try `8-25 frames` at `24-30 fps`.
- Keep short-form gaps below `1 second` unless the pause is intentionally
  cinematic.
- Fade audio edges so the drop does not click.

## QC

- The drop follows a real build.
- The gap does not look like an export error.
- Dialogue re-enters cleanly.
- The reset makes the story or emotion clearer.
