# CapCut Adaptive Texture Captions

Use this preset when social captions should inherit a lively background-reactive
texture while staying readable.

## Pattern

- Generate and correct auto captions.
- Apply style to all only after one caption passes review.
- Use a readable sans font.
- Optional Character spacing: around `-1` for large captions after QC.
- Test Caption Animation presets such as `Single Line`, `Negative Grow`,
  `Multi Line`, or `Multi Line 3`.
- Reposition away from faces, product UI, proof, and designed text.

## QC

- Wording, timing, and line breaks are corrected.
- Captions read on bright, dark, and busy frames.
- Animation finishes before the spoken word needs to be read.
- Ordinary captions are removed where designed text already carries the phrase.
