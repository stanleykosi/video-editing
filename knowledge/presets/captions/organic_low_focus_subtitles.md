# Organic Low-Focus Subtitles

Use this caption style for warm human reels where subtitles should help
comprehension without becoming the main visual event.

## Style

- Short single-line captions when possible.
- Restrained font weight with stroke, shadow, or backing only as needed.
- Static text or soft `3-6 frame` opacity fades.
- No bounce, shake, or aggressive word pops unless a word needs real emphasis.

## QC

- Captions are readable at phone size.
- Captions avoid faces, hands, proof artifacts, and emotional reactions.
- Caption style matches the organic tone.
- Duplicate hook text, proof text, and subtitles are removed unless needed for
  accessibility.
