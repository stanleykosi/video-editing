# Brand Authority Outcome Intro Text

Use this preset for intro text that replaces ordinary captions during a branded
opening bumper.

## Text Roles

- Brand: creator, show, course, community, or series name.
- Proof: quote, community moment, recognized authority, or client/audience proof.
- Outcome: the value the viewer expects to get.
- Accent: underline, highlight, color, or font shift only on the impact phrase.

## QC

- Designed intro text does not duplicate ordinary subtitles.
- One phrase owns the hierarchy at each moment.
- Font mixing has roles; avoid using many random fonts because the intro is fast.
- Proof and outcome text remain readable at phone size.
