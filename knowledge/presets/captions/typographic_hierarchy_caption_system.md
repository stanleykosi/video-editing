# Typographic Hierarchy Caption System

Use this preset when captions need intentional emphasis without becoming a full
motion-graphics stack.

## Settings

- Primary caption font: clean sans or readable serif, matched to the project tone.
- Chunking: phrase-based one-line captions when possible; compact two-line chunks
  only when the phrase needs it.
- Emphasis: one impact word or phrase per caption moment.
- Weight contrast: noticeable jump such as regular to bold or bold to extra-bold.
- Accent color: one brand or meaning color; keep support text neutral.
- Tracking: normal for continuous captions; only slight tightening for large
  emphasis words after phone-size QC.
- Motion: static, soft fade, or short pop that settles before the word is read.

## Use When

- Captions need more hierarchy than plain subtitles.
- Hook or body text should highlight the meaning-changing word.
- The frame already has enough motion and captions should stay readable.

## Avoid When

- Accessibility-first subtitles require full stable lines.
- The screen already has visual text carrying the same idea.
- The style would make serious testimony feel flashy.

## QC

- Emphasis matches the spoken meaning.
- Line breaks form readable phrases.
- Captions avoid faces, products, UI, proof, and CTA text.
- Text remains readable on bright and dark frames.
