# Editorial Warm Paper Palette

Use this palette for documentary explainer graphics with paper texture, maps,
charts, and source highlights.

## Palette Roles

- Base: warm off-white or paper beige.
- Primary text: charcoal or dark gray.
- Highlight: muted yellow or orange.
- Route/alert: red or deep accent.
- Secondary data: muted green, blue, or gray.

## QC

- Text and data remain readable over paper/grid.
- Accent colors do not imply unsupported urgency or hierarchy.
- Warmth does not become orange or muddy after compression.
