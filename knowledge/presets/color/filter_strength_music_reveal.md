# Filter Strength Music Reveal

Use this preset when a filter or look should arrive with the music after speech.

## Pattern

- Speech section with music ducked.
- Filter or adjustment strength at `0`.
- Music restore keyframe after the phrase clears.
- Filter strength rises toward the approved final amount, often up to `100`.
- Hold the full look only during the montage or mood beat.

## QC

- Dialogue is clear before the reveal starts.
- Music restore and filter-strength rise are intentionally aligned or offset.
- Full filter strength preserves captions, faces, proof, UI, products, and brand colors.
- Music is licensed, platform-safe, or temp-marked.
