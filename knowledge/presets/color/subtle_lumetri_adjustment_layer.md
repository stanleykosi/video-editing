# Subtle Lumetri Adjustment Layer

Use this preset pattern for preparing talking-head Reel footage before animation.

## Suggested Controls

- Exposure: small correction only.
- Contrast: small lift if the image is flat.
- Highlights: protect bright skin and background detail.
- Shadows: lower gently only if the image needs depth.
- Whites: avoid clipping.
- Vignette: very subtle, often around 1 or less by eye.

## Rules

- Apply on an adjustment layer so the correction is easy to compare and disable.
- Compare corrected and uncorrected frames before extending the layer across the timeline.
- Do not over-correct before After Effects; preserve skin and detail for animation.
