# Warm Nostalgic Hope Grade

Use this look for hopeful, reflective, memory-driven, or emotional closure
sections where the image should feel warm and less digital.

## Stack

- Base correction for exposure, contrast, white balance, and skin.
- Warm lift or gradient that supports the subject.
- Subtle glow or highlight focus.
- Light grain/noise only if compression remains clean.
- Restrained vignette.
- Optional very light radial blur or softness for nostalgia.

## QC

- Skin remains believable, not orange.
- Highlights are warm but not clipped.
- Captions stay sharp and readable.
- Proof artifacts and faces remain inspectable.
- Grain, blur, and glow do not create muddy compression.
