# YouTube Storytelling Workflow

Use this for from-scratch videos where retention depends on story, not only
information density.
Read `knowledge_plan.md` first so the story structure uses the selected style
pack, skills, lessons, technique cards, and QC checks.

## Story Shapes

- Mystery: question -> clues -> wrong assumption -> reveal -> implication.
- Business history: company/person -> pressure -> decision -> consequence ->
  lesson.
- Sports analysis: moment -> tactical context -> pattern -> key decision ->
  conclusion.
- Documentary explainer: human scene -> system -> evidence -> complication ->
  takeaway.
- Educational short: surprising claim -> mechanism -> example -> practical
  takeaway.

## Hook Rules

- Open with a specific question, contradiction, visual fact, or stakes line.
- Avoid generic "Today we're talking about..." openings.
- Establish why the viewer should care before adding background.
- If using a title card, make it earn its time with curiosity or proof.

## Middle Rules

- Use scene turns, not topic headings.
- Alternate claim, evidence, and implication.
- Add visual proof before asking the viewer to accept a complex explanation.
- Keep each paragraph focused on one story beat.
- Use music and SFX to support turns, not to hide weak structure.

## Ending Rules

- Resolve the question opened by the hook.
- Leave one useful takeaway or emotional aftertaste.
- Avoid a CTA that weakens the final idea.

## QC

- The first 5 seconds open a specific loop.
- Every evidence beat supports the promised story.
- The middle escalates rather than listing.
- The ending resolves the hook.
- Visuals, captions, and sound reinforce the story turn they appear on.
